RuleTap

A rule-based auto-tap accessibility app for Android.

RuleTap is an Accessibility Service tool that watches the text or numbers on your screen, compares them against rules you define, and taps automatically when a match is found.

Get it on Google Play

How it works

  • Define one or more Fields (e.g. price, stock status, location name).
  • For each field, choose a match type: contains, exact match, greater-or-equal, less-than, equal.
  • If more than one field is defined, all of them must match on the same row at the same time.
  • When a match is found, RuleTap runs the tap steps you defined.
  • When no match is found, it can run a step (like tapping "Refresh") and scan again.

Key features

  • Scheduled start/stop and repeat-every-X-minutes automation
  • Draggable floating bubble — control it without switching apps
  • Save/load presets
  • Notification sound on match
  • Adjustable scan interval and humanized tap delay
  • Restricted to only the app you allow (Target App Package Name)

RuleTap isn't tied to any specific site, brand, or service — you define the rule, RuleTap just watches the screen and taps when your condition is met. It does not use an AI model or autonomous decision-making — every action follows the deterministic rule you set.

Who it's for

Anyone who'd rather let RuleTap repeatedly check a screen showing stock, price, availability, or status information instead of checking it by hand.

Permissions & privacy

RuleTap uses Android's Accessibility Service, and that permission is used only to apply the rules you define. See the full Privacy Policy for details on data handling and subscriptions.
