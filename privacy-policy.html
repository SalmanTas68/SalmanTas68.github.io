RuleTap Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the RuleTap Android application ("RuleTap", "the app", "we") handles information. RuleTap is an automation tool that performs actions on your device only according to rules that you define. This policy describes how the app works when subscription functionality is enabled.

2. Who We Are

  • App: RuleTap
  • Package name: com.ruletap.app
  • Privacy contact: ruletap.app@gmail.com

3. Information RuleTap Accesses on Your Device

While an automation run is active, RuleTap uses the Android Accessibility Service to read on-screen information from the app in the foreground, which may include on-screen text and values (such as prices, numbers, place names and durations), content descriptions, the package name of the foreground app, and the position and basic state of on-screen elements. RuleTap uses this information only to compare it with the rules you have defined and to perform the tap actions you have defined. This processing takes place on your device. This information is not sent to RevenueCat or to any RuleTap server.

4. Diagnostic Information

RuleTap keeps a short-term diagnostic log in device memory. It may include text that appeared on screen. You can choose to share a diagnostic report from the Help screen. The report is written to the app's cache folder and passed to the Android share sheet; you choose the destination. Diagnostic reports are never sent automatically.

5. Android ID

RuleTap reads the Android ID (Settings.Secure.ANDROID_ID) on your device and keeps a copy in local storage. It is used only for a local licensing and anti-cloning check on the same device. RuleTap does not send the Android ID to RevenueCat, to RuleTap servers or to any other third party. This statement concerns RuleTap's own transmissions only; Android's device backup, described in section 7, is a separate operating-system mechanism.

6. Subscriptions and RevenueCat

RuleTap uses RevenueCat, a subscription infrastructure service provider, to manage subscriptions and entitlements. When subscription functionality is enabled, the following information may be sent to RevenueCat:

  • an App User ID: a random identifier generated by RuleTap on your device (a UUID). It does not contain your name, email address, phone number or Android ID;
  • subscription, purchase and entitlement information, including the Google Play purchase token and transaction information;
  • technical information about your device and the app, such as device model, device brand, Android API level, app version, package name and the RevenueCat SDK version;
  • as with any internet connection, your IP address may be received by the server you connect to.

Purchases are processed by Google Play. RevenueCat acts as our service provider and processes this information on our behalf. Information is sent over an encrypted connection (TLS). For how RevenueCat handles data, see its privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

RuleTap does not send screen information, rules, diagnostic reports or Android ID to RevenueCat.

7. Local Storage

RuleTap stores the following in its app-private storage on your device: your rules and settings, whether you have accepted the consent screen, a local flag used by the app's access check, the Android ID copy described in section 5, the App User ID described in section 6, and a cached subscription status. This data stays on your device until you change or delete it in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall RuleTap. If Android device backup is enabled on your device, Android may include RuleTap's local storage in your Google account backup, which is governed by Google's backup service.

8. Sharing

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Screen information, rules, diagnostic reports and Android ID are not shared with anyone by the app.
  • Subscription information is shared with RevenueCat and with Google Play as described in section 6.
  • If you choose to use the 'Report a problem' email feature, the email may include your App User ID so that we can identify your subscription account when helping you. This information is sent only when you choose to send the email.
  • We may disclose information if required by law.

9. Retention

  • Diagnostic log: kept in device memory only; cleared when the app's process or service restarts.
  • Diagnostic report file: kept in the app's cache folder; Android's cache management applies.
  • Local app data: until you change or delete it, or uninstall RuleTap.
  • Subscription records held by RevenueCat: retained as described in RevenueCat's privacy policy.

10. Deletion and Your Choices

You can change or remove your rules and settings in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall RuleTap; this removes the data stored on your device. Uninstalling RuleTap does not automatically delete records held by RevenueCat or Google Play. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of information held by RevenueCat, contact RevenueCat at compliance@revenuecat.com, or contact us at ruletap.app@gmail.com and we will help direct your request.

11. Accessibility Service and Automation

RuleTap uses the Android Accessibility Service to perform the automation you configure. RuleTap does not use artificial intelligence or machine-learning models, does not choose targets or actions on its own, and does not record audio, camera or video, does not record keystrokes, and does not intentionally collect passwords or payment card details. You can stop a run at any time and disable the Accessibility Service in Android settings.

12. Children's Privacy

RuleTap is not designed for children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact ruletap.app@gmail.com.

13. Third-Party Apps and Services

RuleTap works with apps installed on your device. You are responsible for complying with the terms of any third-party app or platform you use with RuleTap. RuleTap is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google or any third-party app or service provider.

14. Security

Information stored by RuleTap is kept in the app's private storage, which Android isolates from other apps. Information sent to RevenueCat is sent over an encrypted connection. No system can be guaranteed to be free of risk; keep your device protected with a screen lock and up-to-date software.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. The updated version will be published at this page and the "Last updated" date will be revised.
